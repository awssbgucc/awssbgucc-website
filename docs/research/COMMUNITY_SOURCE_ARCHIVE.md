WEEK 3: FROM LEARNING CLOUD CONCEPTS TO DEPLOYING OUR FIRST WEBSITE ON AWS
Last Saturday, our Zero to Cloud Practitioner Bootcamp participants went beyond learning about AWS services and got their first hands-on deployment experience.

Before the practical session, we explored Compute and Storage, breaking down the concepts with real-life examples to help participants understand how these services fit into everyday applications.

Then came the hands-on lab. By the end of the session, every participant had successfully deployed a Hostel Finder website using Amazon S3, making it accessible on the internet!
It wasn't entirely smooth sailing. Some participants encountered challenges along the way, particularly with certain configurations and bucket policies. But with a bit of troubleshooting and collaborative guidance, everyone crushed the obstacles and achieved the goal.

We wrapped up the day with a lively Kahoot quiz covering everything we've learned over the first three weeks. Huge congratulations to everyone who made it to the podium and took home some well-deserved AWS swag! 👏🏾

What's next?
This coming Saturday, we are moving on to more compute services, exploring Amazon EC2 and AWS Lambda through practical, real-world examples.
And if you've been thinking about joining us or exploring a career in tech, it's not too late, come build with us! https://linktr.ee/awsucc
